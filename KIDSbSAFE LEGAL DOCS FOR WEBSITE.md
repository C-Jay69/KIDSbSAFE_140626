Unlike traditional monitoring tools that operate in secret or blanket-scan private communications, Spykids is engineered for transparency, consent, and targeted safety. Our platform is visible by default, respects age-appropriate autonomy, and focuses exclusively on detecting high-risk behavior like grooming, predatory contact, and exposure to harmful content. We pledge to parents and children that Spykids will:  
✅ Always be visible on the child’s device with clear messaging: *"This app helps Mum/Dad keep you safe online."*

✅ Respect age-based consent: Kids 13+ can opt out (triggers parent alert). Under 13 remains parent-managed.  
✅ Alert only on high-risk signals, leaving normal chats, homework, and friendships private.  
✅ Never sell, share, or monetize child data, location, or message content.
